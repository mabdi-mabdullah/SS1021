Weekly Roadmap (Weeks 01 – 08)

Weekly Roadmap

Week 01: Introduction & Tajweed Fundamentals
Definition and importance of Tajweed, fundamental articulation points (Makhārij), and basic recitation rules.
Week 02: Basic & Famous Words of the Holy Quran
Introduction to basic Quranic vocabulary, Parts of Speech (Ism, Fi‘l, Ḥarf), Masculine vs. Feminine, Singular vs. Plural.
Week 03: Famous Attributes of Allah Mentioned in the Quran
Study and translation of selected Divine Names and Attributes in their Quranic context (Unit 1, Lesson 9).
Week 04: Demonstrative Pronouns (اسْمُ الْإِشَارَة) — هٰذَا and ذٰلِكَ
Near demonstrative (هٰذَا) and distant demonstrative (ذٰلِكَ), identifying the indicated noun (Mushārun Ilayh) in short Ayaat.
Week 05: Particles of Emphasis — إِنَّ and Assertive Lām لَـ
Particle of emphasis (Ḥarf Tawkīd), accusative case effect (Naṣb) on nominal sentences, and double-emphasis (Inna ... la-).
Week 06: Sessional Examination 1
Mid-term evaluation covering vocabulary and grammatical structures from Weeks 01 through 05.
Week 07: Prepositions of Direction and Instrumentality — إِلَىٰ and بِـ
Prepositions (Ḥurūf al-Jarr) and their genitive effect (Jarr / Kasrah) on subsequent nouns (Unit 2, Lessons 6 & 7).
Week 08: The Descriptive Compound (صفت اور موصوف) & Semester Review
Adjective phrases (مرکب توصیفی), 4-way mandatory agreement (gender, number, definiteness, and case), and synthesis for final review.
